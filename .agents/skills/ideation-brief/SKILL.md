# Project Ideation & Brief Synthesizer

This skill directs the agent to brainstorm high-value capstone concepts and produce `02_PROJECT_IDEATION_BRIEF.md` and `03_RESEARCH_DOMAIN_AND_AI.md`.

## Workflow

1. **Ideation & Convergence**:
   - Synthesize the student's profile into 2-3 candidate product ideas combining:
     * **E-Commerce Aspect**: Direct-to-consumer digital storefront, customized merchandising, or virtual try-on.
     * **Immersive Aspect**: Interactive 3D WebXR asset viewer, real-time lighting, spatial rotation, and material customizer.
     * **AI Aspect**: Multimodal AI / Spatial UI (voice-to-scene command, natural language product reconfiguration) or synthetic dataset generator.
   - Select the optimal project concept with documented selection rationale.

2. **Market & Target Group Analysis**:
   - Identify the primary user personas (e.g., modern digital shoppers seeking high visualization fidelity before purchasing).
   - Document explicit pain points (high return rates in online commerce due to inaccurate 2D images, lack of physical context).

3. **Competitive Landscape**:
   - Benchmark against existing market solutions (e.g., Sketchfab 3D Embeds, Shopify 3D AR, Apple Quick Look).
   - Detail advantages, shortcomings, and the unique differentiation of our AI-assisted WebXR workflow.

4. **AI vs. Non-AI Functional Matrix**:
   - Classify all system modules into:
     * **Traditional Deterministic Logic**: 3D rendering loop, camera matrices, e-commerce cart calculation.
     * **AI-Augmented Logic**: Natural language command parser (LLM), semantic color/material mapping, automated synthetic dataset capture.

5. **Output**:
   Write the findings into `docs/02_PROJECT_IDEATION_BRIEF.md` and `docs/03_RESEARCH_DOMAIN_AND_AI.md`.
